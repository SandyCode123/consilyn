Terms of use

Consilyn is free software for Windows. By installing or using it you agree to these terms.

What it is

It is a screen that listens to your side and the other side of a call on your own laptop, turns the speech into text there, and suggests answers you might give. The suggestions come from Google's Gemini service, using your own Gemini key.

Your responsibilities

  • Consent. You must tell everyone on the call that a tool is transcribing it, and go ahead only if they agree. The law on this differs by country and by US state, and some places require everyone's consent. The app asks you to confirm this before it listens, but whether you have really done it is your responsibility.
  • Your network's rules. Expert networks and clients may forbid assistant or transcription tools on their calls. Check before you use it, and follow their terms.
  • What you say. Suggestions can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Anything not drawn from your own material is marked not verified. You decide what you say, and you are responsible for it. Never share confidential information or material non-public information, whatever the screen suggests.
  • Your Gemini key and account. Your use of Gemini is governed by Google's terms. On Gemini's free tier Google may use the text sent to it to improve its products, and people may review it.

Free calls and paid plans

  • During early access every call is free. After that, the free calls are the first 5, then 2 each calendar month. The free allowance and the price may change, and the app shows the current numbers before each call.
  • To count free calls, the app asks our server before each call. If the server can't be reached, the call still goes ahead and is counted later. A third call in a row started offline asks you to connect first.
  • A paid plan is a monthly subscription, sold by Dodo Payments as merchant of record. It renews until you cancel, and cancelling stops the next renewal. Payments are not refundable, except where the law requires it (see REFUND.md). Please use the free calls to try it first. A licence key works on up to 2 laptops at a time; "Move to another laptop" in Set up frees one.
  • Trying to get around the free-call count, for example by changing the app, is not allowed.
