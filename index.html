Privacy

Your call's audio never leaves your laptop.

  • No recording. Speech becomes text on your laptop and the audio is never saved.
  • Only text goes out, to Google's Gemini with your own key, to write the answers. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use that text to improve its products.
  • No account, no cookies. Our server only counts free calls and never sees anything said.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does Consilyn record my calls?

No. Speech is turned into text on your laptop a few seconds at a time and the audio is thrown away. No sound file is ever saved, and audio never leaves your laptop.

Where do the answers come from?

Google's Gemini writes them from your screening answers, your background and what has been said on the call. Where an answer uses your own material, your exact words are quoted underneath.

Can it contradict what I submitted?

Each answer is checked against your screening answers before it appears. If it disagrees, your own screening answer is shown instead. You always decide what you say.

How fast are the answers?

Answers prepared before the call appear about three seconds after the client stops talking; others take a few seconds more, with a line to start on while you wait. A paid Gemini key is fastest; the free one has daily limits.

Is what I say used to train AI?

Our server never sees anything said. Text goes to Google's Gemini with your own key. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use that text to improve its products; on a paid tier it does not train on it.

Which call apps does it work with?

Zoom and Google Meet in Chrome work best. Consilyn follows whichever app is on the call, so others such as Microsoft Teams can work too.

Do I need an account?

No. You need a free Gemini key from Google, and the app shows you where to get one the first time it starts.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is it safe?

Yes. The installer is new and not yet code-signed, so Windows asks first. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The very first start takes a few minutes while Windows checks the new files.

What does it cost?

Every call is free during early access. After that, your first 5 calls are free, then 2 free calls every month, or unlimited calls for $39 a month.

Does it work on a Mac, or in other languages?

Not yet. Consilyn is for Windows 10 and 11, in English.
