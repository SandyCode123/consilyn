## What it is

It is a screen that listens to your side and the other side of a call on your own
laptop, turns the speech into text there, and suggests answers you might give. The
suggestions come from Google's Gemini service, using your own Gemini key.

- During early access every call is free. After that, the free calls are the
  first 5, then 2 each calendar month. The free allowance and the price may
  change, and the app shows the current numbers before each call.
- To count free calls, the app asks our server before each call. If the server
  can't be reached, the call still goes ahead and is counted later. A third call
  in a row started offline asks you to connect first.
