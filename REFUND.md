# Refund policy

**Payments are not refundable.**

You can try Consilyn before you pay: every new laptop gets free calls,
and the app shows how many are left before each call. Please use them to check it
works with your laptop, headset and call app before you buy.

- **Subscriptions renew monthly until you cancel.** Cancelling stops the next
  renewal. You keep unlimited calls until the end of the month you've paid for. No
  part of a month is refunded.
- **Payments are processed by Dodo Payments**, the merchant of record. Where the law
  where you live gives you a right to a refund, that right still applies, and Dodo
  Payments handles it.
- **To cancel, or for a question about a payment**, use the link in your purchase
  email, or write to hello@consilyn.com.
